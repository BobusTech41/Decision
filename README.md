# DECISION - Born in Indonesia | Developed by a high-school student from Indonesia

### A low-res 3D game about decision-based outcomes in life.
### This is an offline game so no internet required.
### There is no saving system in this game so user will play until the story ends and will reset on next launch.
### This game is fully developed with python and may include other libraries.

###### At the start of the game, you choose to be born into one of three families: poor, middle-class, or rich.
###### Poor: Your family has a low income and lives in a cheap, insect-infested apartment on the edge of the city.
###### Middle-class: Your family earns a moderate income, owns a car, and sends you to a decent private school in Indonesia.
###### Rich: You are born into an old-money family that owns a highly successful company, and you attend the most prestigious international school in Indonesia.
